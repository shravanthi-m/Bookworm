1 · Home & level not run

arm: ?
Goes home, then reads the table's tilt in the depth image. The tilt is removed from depth in software.

2 · First capture not run

One photo from home; the book's outline gives six points: four corners and the centre of each page.

3 · Depth scan not run

The camera visits six spots around home at home height (never lower) and fuses the depth views into the page's height above the table.

4 · Z link not run

table z in the arm frame
-
Lowers the tip in 2 mm steps onto bare table at least 80 mm from the book, reading the joint load after every step. The spot is the bare table farthest from the book, out at the edge of what the camera sees (it is locked after the first touch). When the load jumps the arm goes straight back one step (and home, if the load stays high); that height is the table's z. Then up, across, and the same on the bottom-left corner of the page (BL, 15 mm in from the edge) as a check. Charts: real tip z per step against the fitted trend; below, how far each step is off the trend for position and load. Every run is saved in data/depth/zlink/.

5 · Book model not run

model file
No motion. The depth map's page heights, turned into arm z by the z link, and corrected by every place the tip has really touched the page (1-2 touches: a constant shift; 3 or more spread out: a tilted plane). The demo touches where this model puts the page, and its touches are merged back in afterwards (v2, v3, ...). Saved in data/book/: book_model.json + book_model.npz (the grid).

6 · XY marking (manual) not run

XY correction in use
-
Optional, needs you at the table; not part of Run all. Put paper you can mark on the book if needed. 1) Start: the arm hovers the tip 3 mm above each point in turn (aimed with the XY correction in use). 2) Mark the paper exactly under the fingertip with a pen, keep your hand clear, press "Marked". 3) After the last point the arm goes home and takes a photo: open the viewer's "XY marks", pick each point and click its mark. 4) Check the table (how far each landing was from the aim) and press Save: the mean becomes part of the XY correction the demo aims with. Saved in data/calibration/.

7 · Touch demo not run

First the bare table far from the book, at the table's z; then each point, back to back, at exactly the page height the book model gives (corners 15 mm in from the edge). Between points the arm goes straight up to half the home height, across, and straight down. If the load jumps before that height, the page is higher than thought: the arm backs off at once and the difference is reported.
